\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

Sampling bearing vibration below the Nyquist rate by pure decimation brings the fault resonance into
a narrow band, and with it whatever impulsive content lies in the folded zones.
\begin{itemize}
  \item On Ottawa, impulsive content in the folded zones, a train of single-sample spikes present in
  healthy records too, decided whether blind band selection survived the rate reduction, and
  injected impulses did the same on CWRU, where a surrogate with the same folded spectrum did not.
  An anti-alias filter at the reduced rate avoided the capture at no cost on Ottawa; removing
  isolated outliers in the decimated stream, a three-point median or a lower search ceiling avoided
  it at $4\times$ without access to the full rate.
  \item On Paderborn the selector was captured before any decimation. A decision restricted to the
  fault orders, with a threshold from healthy bearings, recovered much of what the blind pipeline
  missed at every rate up to 1/16, though only 12 points at 1/32, and the advantage was already
  present at full rate. An anti-alias filter before decimation did better than pure decimation.
  \item Learned sampling masks did not outperform trained uniform decimation or random masks, but
  the intervals on Paderborn do not exclude a gain of 5 points. The decoders that the masks shared
  gained over the classical pipeline only on the bearings where the kurtogram was captured.
\end{itemize}

Each benchmark suggests one check for a node that samples below the Nyquist rate. Before choosing
between pure decimation and filtering, measure the accuracy by Nyquist zone on records with known
faults, since the answer differed between benchmarks. After decimation, protect the band choice or
the decision, since the protection that the anti-alias filter gives at full rate is gone. Before
training learned acquisition on a benchmark, check that the planned test can detect a gain. The
results model an ideal sampler. The bandwidth of a real track-and-hold, which decides what folds,
has to be measured on the target converter.
